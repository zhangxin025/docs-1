**Can I create a full-body avatar?**

Yes! Just upload a full-body photo or video that meets our quality requirements (resolution, size, format, etc.).

------

**What should I avoid in my photo or video?**

Avoid face obstructions (especially the mouth), extreme head tilts (over 45°), or other people in the frame.

------

**Can I use an AI-generated photo?**

Yes, as long as it’s a clear JPG or PNG (max 10MB, 1080P), with a single, visible face—no mouth covered, and head tilted under 45°.

------

**What photo formats are supported?**

JPG or PNG, up to 10MB, with resolution of 1920×1080 or 1080×1920.

------

**Can I use a photo without uploading a voice clip?**

No. A 5–10 second voice clip is required with a photo. If you don’t have one, you can use a preset voice, but that voice will be used in your avatar’s videos. For the best results, we recommend recording your own in a quiet space.

------

**Why do you need an audio clip for photo-based avatars?**

To clone your voice, we need at least 5 seconds of clear, natural speech. You can also choose a default voice instead.

------

**What video formats are supported?**

MP4 or MOV. Recommended resolution: 1080p–4K. Max file size: 1GB. If the video’s too long, we’ll auto-trim it to a 1-minute preview so you can select your 5-second clip.

------

**Why do you only use a 5-second video clip?**

We use a short, natural speaking clip to speed up avatar creation and give you a smoother experience. Choose a clip that feels conversational.

------

**Can I request specific movements for my avatar?** 

Not at this stage. To keep things fast and simple, we only support basic expressions based on your video. For custom actions, contact us about advanced options.

------

**Can I change the avatar's background or make it transparent?**

No, background replacement isn’t supported. Please record your video with the background you want to appear in the final output.

------

**Does the training video need to include audio?**

No. If no audio is detected, we’ll ask you to upload or record one, or you can pick a default voice from our library.

------

**Can I use a singing clip as my audio?**

No. Since the avatar is used for natural speech, we need a voice clip with regular spoken language.

------

**How long does avatar training take?**

About 15–20 minutes with a stable internet connection.

------

**Where can I check my avatar’s status?**

In your **[**[**Avatar Library - Personal**](https://duix.com/platform/avatars)**]** section.

------
