# AI Avatar Training Made Easy

Create your very own AI Avatar in just minutes—with a simple video or photo. It’s quick, fun, and requires zero tech skills!

### What’s This About?

To bring your Personal AI Avatar to life, we just need a short video or a clear photo from you. The better the quality, the more lifelike your Avatar will be—capturing your face and voice for natural, realistic conversations.

Click **[[Create Avatars](https://duix.com/platform/replicas/create)]** to upload your training video or photo.
Training usually takes about **15–20 minutes**.
Check the status in **[[Avatar Library - Personal](https://duix.com/platform/avatars)]** or enable your Avatar from **[[Mine](https://duix.com/platform/create)]** when creating conversation.

------

# How to Record a Short Training Video (10 sec – 1 min)

Want the most realistic result? A short video is the best way to go.

**What to do:**

- Act like you're having a natural, real-life conversation.
- Keep the video between **10 seconds and 1 minute**, with at least **5 seconds of clear speech**.
- Make sure **only your face** appears—no one else.
- Avoid covering your face: no mics, hands, or objects in the way.
- Keep your head mostly straight (no tilting more than 45°), and please—no facial hair for this part.
- Save your video in **MP4 or MOV** format. We recommend **1080p to 4K resolution**, up to **1GB** in size.

We’ll help you pick the perfect 5-second snippet for voice cloning and facial mapping.

------

# How to Submit a Photo (with Voice)

No time for video? A single high-quality photo works too—just pair it with a short voice clip.

**What to do:**

- Upload one **clear photo of your face**, no obstructions.
- Only **your face** should appear—no extra people.
- Avoid anything that blocks the mouth, face, or neck.
- Head should be mostly straight (within 45°), and again, no facial hair.
- Use **JPG or PNG** format, max **10MB**, resolution **1920×1080** or **1080×1920**.
- Also upload a **5–10 second audio file** with clear speech for voice cloning.

------

#  What Happens Next?

Once you upload your file, your Avatar starts training right away. After **15–20 minutes**, check your results under **[[Avatar Library - Personal](https://duix.com/platform/avatars)]**.

Not happy with the outcome? No worries—just reach out to us and we’ll help you get it right.
