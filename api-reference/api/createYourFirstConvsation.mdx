## Overview

This guide walks you through creating your first **conversation** using the **Duix Platform** and preparing it for SDK or API integration.

---

## Step 1: Create an Account

To use the Duix API, first create a [**Duix**](https://duix.com) account.  
Click [**Go to create**](https://duix.com/login) to register and sign in.

![Login Page](/images/login.png)

---

## Step 2: Access the Duix Workspace

After logging in, click the **My Duix** icon in the upper-right corner of the page to open your **workspace**.

---

## Step 3: Create a Conversation

You can create a new conversation manually or by using a **predefined template**.

> ### **Template Character**
> 
> - **LLM & TTS/ASR** — Use Duix’s default configuration or connect your own third-party API keys.  
> - **Character Name** — The display name shown when the avatar joins the conversation.  
> - **Conversation Context** — Provide personality traits, background stories, or any relevant text materials.  
> - **Avatar** — Assign a default virtual avatar to represent the character.

> ### **Custom Character**
>
> - **LLM & TTS/ASR** — Configure the platform defaults or integrate third-party services.  
> - **Character Prompt** — Define the character’s personality, tone, and behavior style.  
> - **Conversation Context** — Supply narrative context or detailed background information.  
> - **First Message** — The opening line spoken by the character.  
> - **Error Message** — The fallback response used when LLM or TTS/ASR processing fails.  
> - **Avatar** — Assign a specific avatar for the character.

---

## Step 4: Retrieve Your Credentials

After creating a conversation, copy the following information from your **dashboard**:

- **App ID**  
- **App Key**  
- **Conversation ID**

Click the **Copy** icon next to each field to copy it to your clipboard, then paste it into your project configuration.

> ⚠️ **Security Notice:**  
> Do **not share your App Key**. It grants full access to your account and should be kept confidential.

![Start Conversation](/images/startconverstion.png)

---

## Step 5: Begin Integration

Once your conversation is created, you can begin testing with the **Duix SDK** or **API** to complete the full workflow.  
Refer to the [**API Documentation**](https://docs.duix.com/) for detailed integration guides.
