## Overview

The conversation ID is a unique identifier that links your API calls to a specific Interactive AI avatar conversation. You'll need this ID to programmatically interact with your avatar through the Duix API.

## How to Get Your Conversation ID

Follow these simple steps to retrieve your conversation ID from the dashboard:

### Step 1: Locate Your Avatar Card

Navigate to your Interactive AI avatar dashboard and find the avatar card you want to work with.

### Step 2: Copy the Conversation ID

![](/images/getConversationID.png)

1. Look for the **Copy ID** button in the bottom-right corner of your Interactive AI avatar card
2. Click **Copy ID** to copy the conversation ID to your clipboard

<Tip>
The conversation ID is automatically copied to your clipboard when you click the Copy ID button, making it easy to paste into your API calls or code.
</Tip>
